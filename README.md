## 🏛️ The Five Royal Salons

### 1. ⚜ L'Astrolabe Céleste Royal (The Celestial Astrolabe)
- Fully interactive 16th-century brass astrolabe drawn in high-precision HTML5 Canvas.
- Rotatable rete featuring classical star pointers (Sirius, Vega, Aldebaran, Rigel, Arcturus, Antares).
- Real-time astronomical computations: solar altitude, zodiacal positioning, and sidereal hours.
- Interchangeable latitude plates for Cordoba (37°N), Paris (48°N), London (51°N), Alexandria (31°N), and Mecca (21°N).

### 2. 📜 La Loupe du Bibliophile & Manuscrits Anciens (The Illuminated Folio)
- High-resolution illuminated manuscript page on warm vellum/parchment with gilded drop caps.
- Realistic brass magnifying glass (loupe de lecture) following cursor movement with optical distortion and atmospheric lighting.
- Featuring historical reflections from Ibn Al-Haytham (*Kitāb al-Manāẓir*), Sir Isaac Newton (*Principia Mathematica*), and René Descartes.

### 3. 🧭 Cartographia Antiqua (The Portolan Maritime Atlas)
- 17th-century style nautical sea chart with radiating golden rhumb lines and a mathematical 32-point compass rose.
- Interactive chronicle of three legendary global voyages:
  1. *Ibn Battuta's Epic Journey (1325–1354)*
  2. *Captain James Cook’s Pacific Voyage on HMS Endeavour (1768)*
  3. *Jean-François de La Pérouse French Royal Expedition (1785)*

### 4. ⏳ L'Échappement Horloger de Précision (The Royal Escapement)
- Real-time physical simulation of George Graham and Abraham-Louis Breguet's deadbeat anchor escapement.
- Gilded escape wheel with ratchet teeth, blued steel pallet arms, and swinging brass pendulum.
- Acoustically synchronized ticking mechanism generated live via Web Audio API.

### 5. 🖋️ Scriptorium Regium & Le Sceau Royal (Illuminated Certificate Folio)
- Bespoke illuminated certificate generator with custom wax seal, imperial calligraphy, and instant high-resolution printing for scholars and visitors.

## 🎨 Visual Design Philosophy

- **Vellum & Parchment:** Warm ivory and aged paper tones (`#fbf7ee`, `#f4ecdc`, `#d8c4a0`).
- **Heraldic Palette:** Cardinal red wax seals (`#861819`), British racing green (`#0d281e`), French Bordeaux (`#3d0c14`), and antique leaf gold (`#c99a3e`).
- **Typography:** *Cinzel Decorative*, *Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, paired with classical Arabic typography *Amiri* and *Aref Ruqaa*.
- **Zero Plastic UI:** Strict avoidance of generic modern emoji-heavy cards or flat buttons. Everything is rendered with classical filigree, engraved brass, and embossed wax seals.
